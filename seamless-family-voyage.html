Rare Experiences

Places That
Few Will Ever Know

Beyond the known — our curators source experiences accessible only to those with the right connections.

🏝️

Maldives

Private Sandbank Dinners

An uninhabited sandbank set with lanterns, a private chef, and your family — the entire ocean as your dining room under a canopy of stars.

🐘

Thailand · Chiang Rai

Ethical Elephant Sanctuary

A private dawn visit to a sanctuary before other guests arrive. Walk, bathe, and bond with rescued elephants in the misty northern highlands.

🌿

Bali · Ubud

Rice Paddy Sunrise Yoga

A dawn practice above the Tegallalang terraces with a master yogi, followed by a Balinese blessing ceremony at a centuries-old water temple.

🚢

Thailand · Phang Nga Bay

Overnight Junk Charter

Sleep aboard a traditional wooden junk as it anchors in hidden coves. Kayak into sea caves at dusk. Breakfast served on deck as mist lifts off the karsts.

🤿

Maldives · Hanifaru Bay

Manta Ray Dusk Swim

A UNESCO protected bay where mantas gather in numbers that can exceed 200 — witnessed at dusk on a private guided snorkel with marine biologists.

🎭

Bali · Seminyak

Kecak Fire Dance at Clifftop

Witness the ancient fire dance at Uluwatu temple as the sun sets over the Indian Ocean — arranged with private reserved seating and a local guide.

🍃

Sri Lanka · Sinharaja

Rainforest Night Safari

The world's only wet-zone biosphere reserve, explored by torchlight with a naturalist. Rare endemic birds, walking ferns, and the symphony of the jungle after dark.

🛶

Thailand · Khlong Lat Mayom

Floating Market by Longtail

Not the tourist version — a locals-only floating market accessed at dawn by private longtail boat, where vendors have traded along these canals for generations.
